Build an interactive AI-powered application called “Career Path Builder – From Aspiration to an Achievable Plan”.

PURPOSE:
The application should help students convert their education and career aspirations into a clear, realistic, personalized, and achievable career plan.

USER INPUTS:
Ask the student to provide:
1. Name
2. Age
3. Current education/course
4. Career aspiration or dream job
5. Areas of interest
6. Current skills
7. Skills they want to improve
8. Academic strengths
9. Academic weaknesses
10. Available study time per day or week
11. Preferred learning method

GENERATE THE FOLLOWING:

1. CAREER GOAL ANALYSIS
Analyze the student's career aspiration and explain:
- What the career involves
- Required qualifications
- Important skills
- Possible career opportunities

2. PERSONALIZED CAREER ROADMAP
Create a step-by-step roadmap from the student's current education level to their desired career.

3. SKILL DEVELOPMENT PLAN
Recommend:
- Technical skills
- Soft skills
- Certifications
- Practical projects
- Important knowledge areas

4. EDUCATION AND LEARNING PLAN
Suggest suitable subjects, courses, certifications, projects, and learning activities related to the student's career goal.

5. SMART GOALS
Convert the student's career ambition into Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound goals.

6. ACTION PLAN
Create achievable goals for:
- Next 3 months
- Next 6 months
- Next 1 year
- Long-term career development

7. WEEKLY STUDY PLAN
Create a simple weekly learning schedule based on the student's available study time.

8. PROGRESS TRACKER
Create a checklist of milestones that the student can complete and track.

9. CHALLENGES AND SOLUTIONS
Identify possible challenges such as:
- Lack of confidence
- Lack of resources
- Time management
- Lack of skills
- Difficulty staying motivated

Provide practical solutions for each challenge.

10. CAREER GUIDANCE CHAT
Provide an interactive career guidance section where students can ask questions about their career, education, skills, learning plan, and future opportunities.

OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS:
Make all recommendations personalized according to the student's answers.

Use simple, clear, encouraging, and student-friendly language.

Use headings, bullet points, checklists, tables, and timelines wherever appropriate.

Do not make unrealistic promises or guarantee jobs, salaries, or career success.

The complete student journey should follow:

ASPIRATION
↓
SELF-ASSESSMENT
↓
CAREER GOAL
↓
EDUCATION PLAN
↓
SKILL DEVELOPMENT
↓
SMART GOALS
↓
ACTION PLAN
↓
PROGRESS TRACKING
↓
ACHIEVEMENT

The final application should be simple, modern, easy to understand, and useful for students.
